# Teach

1. Establish the learner's goal, prior knowledge, and practical mission.
2. Gather trustworthy primary resources and record them.
3. Teach one tightly scoped concept at a time.
4. Use retrieval questions and an immediate feedback loop.
5. Save concise references and learning records in the current teaching workspace.
6. Adjust the next lesson based on demonstrated understanding, not assumed fluency.

Keep lessons short, concrete, cited, and connected to the learner's stated purpose. Ask before changing the mission or durable learning records.
